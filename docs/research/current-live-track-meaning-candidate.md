# Current live-track meaning candidate

The observed Electron session identified the track as `JANE DOE` by Kenshi
Yonezu. Official release material identifies it as the ending theme for *Chainsaw
Man – The Movie: Reze Arc* ([Sony Music release notice](https://ssl.sme.co.jp/artist/utadahikaru/info/577392),
[official film music page](https://chainsawman.dog/movie_reze/music/)). An interview
translation reports that the song's central image involves walking barefoot over
shattered glass and red traces, and explains the title as an unidentified woman
([interview translation](https://vgperson.com/vocalinterview.php?view=hachiwired1991)).

These are useful research leads, not yet a project `SongMeaning`: they are not
timestamped lyric evidence supplied to the sidecar. The live renderer should
therefore remain abstained until the lyrics/transcript are verified and compiled
into motifs, relations, and section actions. This is deliberate evidence gating,
not a missing prompt adjective.
